On your phone, use the big buttons beside the board. Hold an arrow to move repeatedly. You can hold a piece once before locking the next one.

Touch down. Then turn. A piece stays movable for 0.8 seconds after landing. Rotate or slide to restart that time, up to 15 adjustments. The green landing bar shows the time left. Wall and floor kicks help pieces turn in tight spaces. Hard drop locks immediately.

Consecutive clears, four-line clears, T-spins, and all-clear boards earn bonuses.
