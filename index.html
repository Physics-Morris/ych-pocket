A push. A drift. A little luck.

Press the yellow buttons to send a little jet of water through the tank. Let the rings drift down over the pink posts. Catch all twelve.

Use both thumbs on a phone, or A / D and the arrow keys on a keyboard. Each press gives one little push.

Use the toolbar above the toy to change the SCENE, toggle SOUND, or RESET for a fresh handful of rings.

Make it a pocket toy.On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Launch YCH Pocket from its new Home Screen icon and turn your phone sideways.
MORE TOY. LESS BROWSER.

Full screen on iPhone.

  1. Open this game in Safari and tap Share.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  3. Open YCH Pocket using its new Home Screen icon.
  4. Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center and turn your iPhone sideways.

Opening from the Home Screen removes Safari’s address bar and tabs. Rotating a regular Safari tab doesn’t automatically enter full screen.
