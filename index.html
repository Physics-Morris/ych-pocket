JUST LIKE YOU REMEMBER

A push. A drift. A little luck.

Choose Classic or Color Match, then press START to begin the timer. Classic accepts rings on any pink post. Color Match only counts a ring on its same-colored post.

Press the left yellow button to push rings up and right; the right pushes up and left. Use both thumbs, A / D, or the arrow keys. Caught rings get a little heavier. They can still slide up and blow off a post with repeated pushes, reducing your score!

Turn FISH ON before starting to bring the three background fish to life. They swim left and right, rise and dive, and occasionally peck rings. When the round stops, they return and blend back into the scenery. Mode and fish settings stay fixed during a round; RESET returns to setup.

Catch all twelve at once to stop the timer. Your final scene stays visible. Enter your name above the toy and tap SAVE, then open TIMES to see the fastest ten on this device. Each mode and fish setting has a separate board. The controls tuck away during play so the toy can fill more of the screen. Tap MENU to pause and show them, then RESUME to continue. The timer also pauses while settings are open or the page is hidden.

Tap TILT to steer with real gravity. Lower the top edge to lift rings and the bottom edge to settle them. Change the SCENE or toggle SOUND above the toy.
A LITTLE TWIST. A LITTLE GRAVITY.

Steer the water.

Real gravity. On by default. Lower an edge of your phone and the rings move that way. Rotate like a steering wheel to guide them left and right. Tip the top edge down to lift them, then lower the bottom edge to settle them onto the three posts.

Hold your phone sideways and enable tilt. No calibration needed.

Allow motion access if your phone asks. No installation needed. The yellow pumps work in both modes. Caught rings can lift off their posts while the round is running.
MORE TOY. LESS BROWSER.

More room to play.

Stay in Safari. No installation needed.

  1. Open this game in Safari. Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock and turn your iPhone sideways.
  2. Tap Safari’s Page Menu beside the address bar (aA on older iOS versions).
  3. Choose Hide Toolbar. If it isn’t listed, check the menu’s More (…) options.

This hides Safari’s controls for more playing space. Tap the address-bar area to bring them back. iPhone Safari doesn’t let this game switch a regular tab into true full screen automatically.

Optional: add a Home Screen shortcut
  1. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  3. Launch YCH Pocket from its new icon and rotate sideways to play without Safari’s toolbar.
